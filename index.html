I've developed several products, both for the App Store and the web, including:

  • ItsMee: A platform that empowers every creator on social media to have their own AI, allowing their fans to interact with their AI. On ItsMee anyone can create an AI chatbot of any creator on Instagram, LinkedIn, and X (Twitter), And have a conversation with that chatbot.
  • Bang: An app that helps people express themselves through music using generative AI. On Bang, you can watch short videos with music playing in the background. As you spend more time on Bang every week, we create three personalized music for you. You can listen to them and use them in your Bang videos to express yourself through music.
  • Younme: Younme is a social app that helps people connect with each other in the real world. Every morning, Users are randomly matched with another user in their city called "UserOfTheDay". The app provides the name of a public place in the city, called Younme Place, Where the two users can meet. Users can then text each other within the app to plan their meetup. Once they meet in person, Users can take a photo with their UserOfTheDay and share it in the app. The photo could be of anything, Such as the two users having coffee together or simply hanging out in the Younme Place. After the meetup, Users can upvote or downvote their UserOfTheDay. They can also add the person as a friend. If the user does not add their UserOfTheDay as a friend, All messages between them will disappear the next day.
