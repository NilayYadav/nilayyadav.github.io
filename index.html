Over time, I've worked on numerous products based on my ideas for both the App Store and the web, some of which include:

  • Pegasus: I'm currently working on Pegasus - An AI assistant that enables people to use natural language to interact with other apps. You can ask Leo to buy something from Amazon, order Uber Eats, fill out forms, and perform tasks that you would typically do manually by navigating various apps. This assistant would learn to use other apps in a manner similar to how humans do.

  • Qurio: Qurio - It's a tool that reads your mind, discerns the questions in your thoughts, and provides answers for you, eliminating the need for you to type elsewhere to find the answer.

  • ItsMee: ItsMee is a platform that empowers every creator on social media to have their own AI, allowing their fans to interact with their AI. Where people can make AI chatbots for creators, So their fans can chat like it's them! unlike other AI like ChatGPT, ItsMee knows that particular creator and their world.

  • Bang: An app designed to help people express themselves through music using generative AI. On Bang, users can watch short videos with personalized music, creating a unique and experience This AI allows fans to interact with their favorite creators in a unique way.

  • Younme: A social app facilitating real-world connections. Users are randomly matched with a "UserOfTheDay" in their city, and the app suggests a meeting place. After the meetup, users can share their experiences through photos, messaging, upvote/downvote, and potentially become friends.

  • Nova: It's a new kind of browser + answer engine that provides direct answers while web surfing. It's not limited to a chat interface like Perplexity and Bard, and it doesn't give the same answers. But instead give answers according to the web page
